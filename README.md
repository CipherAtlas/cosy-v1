## Shared village

The outdoor village follows one shared interaction contract, recorded in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md#shared-village-interaction-rules). The Worker accepts ownership, seat and task requests before the browser applies them. An engaged animal or resident remains with its visitor; other visitors can observe but cannot take or restart it. Benches and swings have their actual seat capacity, and a full bench may refuse entry. Sitting faces the front of the bench regardless of approach direction, including the birdwatching bench. Outdoor tasks share availability, resources and action clocks. Leaving, disconnecting and expired reservations release abandoned claims. Every visitor's focus cottage is independently available: its interior, furniture, cat, timer and local actions are private, with only that visitor's own companions visible. Future interactions must preserve this contract and be checked with multiple clients.
