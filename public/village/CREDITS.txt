Garden and pond kit (2026-09-27) — original project artwork.
17 models authored in Blender: flowers, sunflower, mint, vegetables, reeds,
lily, swan, duck, duckling, fish, watering can, basket, bread pouch and teapot.
Mint v2 adds original serrated green leaves and veins; bed signs and countdowns are authored in the runtime.
Editable source: assets/village/garden-pond.blend. Runtime: models/garden-pond.glb.
Named pivots are animated by the village runtime; no third-party models or textures.
Planting, watering, picking, pouring, bread crumbs, splashes and duck calls are
original procedural Web Audio effects. Existing recorded soundtrack credits above apply.

White dove and bird clearing (2026-09-27) — original project artwork.
Dove authored in Blender 5.2.1 LTS: assets/village/dove.blend;
runtime models/dove.glb; generator scripts/village/create_dove.py.
White plumage, scalloped wings, rosy cheeks and tiny peach feet use vertex colors.
Wren uses the original spirit with original flower/scarf/pouch accessories.
No third-party models or textures. The two-note coo is an original Web Audio effect.

Village puppies (2026-09-29) — original Cosy project artwork.
Mochi, Kiko, Biscuit and Cloud modeled in Blender 5.2.1 LTS.
Editable source: assets/village/puppies.blend; generator: scripts/village/create_puppies.py.
Runtime model: models/puppies.glb. No third-party character models or textures.
