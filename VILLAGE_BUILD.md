The default 2.65 m chain can lift the seat about 2.10 m above its resting position. Seats stay level, the blob stays seated, and the camera frames the whole arc from a steady diagonal view. Frame caps prevent long-tab catch-up. This is constrained pendulum physics, not a general rigid-body or deformable-chain solver.
